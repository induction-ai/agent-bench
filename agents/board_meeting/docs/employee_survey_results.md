# Meridian Technologies — Employee Engagement Survey Results

**Survey Period:** June 1-14, 2026
**Conducted by:** Culture Amp (third-party survey platform)
**Response Rate:** 81% (723 of 891 employees)
**Prepared by:** VP of People, Jessica Thornton
**Classification:** Board Confidential

---

### Engineering (338 respondents, 89% response rate)

| Category                | Score | Change vs Q4 2025 |

| Overall Engagement      | 3.4   | -0.7              |
| Manager Effectiveness   | 3.6   | -0.5              |

| Compensation & Benefits | 3.5   | -0.3              |
| Company Direction       | 3.3   | -0.9              |
| eNPS                    | +2    | -28               |

**Engineering is the most concerning department.** The Work-Life Balance score of 2.6 is the lowest score in any category in any department since we began surveying. Engineering eNPS collapsed from +30 to +2 year-over-year.

**Top themes from open-ended responses (Engineering):**

1. **"Burnout from Atlas 4.0 crunch"** (mentioned by 67% of engineering respondents)
   - "We've been in crunch mode since January. The May deadline was unrealistic from the start."
   - "I worked 14 weekends in Q2. My family is suffering."
   - "The security vulnerability remediation in May meant another 3 weeks of all-hands-on-deck after we thought we were done."

2. **"Unclear product direction without VP of Product"** (mentioned by 54%)
   - "We're building features but nobody is clearly prioritizing them. Anil is doing his best but he's also the CTO."
   - "I've had 3 different stakeholders tell me conflicting priorities for Atlas 4.1 in the same week."
   - "Janet's departure left a vacuum that hasn't been filled. The two PMs who followed her took institutional knowledge with them."

3. **"Concerned about technical debt from 4.0 rush"** (mentioned by 41%)
   - "We cut corners to ship. The auth middleware rewrite was done in 4 weeks when it should have been 8."
   - "There are TODO comments in production code. That's never happened at Meridian before."
   - "The test coverage for the new streaming engine is 62%. Our standard is 85%."

4. **"Want more recognition"** (mentioned by 29%)
   - "We shipped the biggest release in company history and the all-hands focused on the delay, not the achievement."
   - "No bonuses, no extra PTO, no acknowledgment that people sacrificed their weekends for months."

### Notable Departures (Q2)

- **Janet Liu** — VP of Product (April 18). Departed for Databricks as SVP of Product. Cited "philosophical differences about product strategy" in exit interview.
- **Alex Tran** — Senior PM, Atlas (May 2). Followed Janet Liu to Databricks. Cited "loss of confidence in product leadership."
- **Michelle Dubois** — Senior PM, Enterprise Features (May 8). Departed for Stripe. Cited "burnout and better WLB opportunity."
- **Kevin Park** — Staff Engineer, Streaming (June 12). Departed for Pinnacle Systems. Stated in exit interview that he was "recruited directly by Pinnacle's CTO" and was offered a 35% comp increase. Kevin was a key contributor to the Atlas 4.0 streaming engine.

**Concern:** Kevin Park's departure to Pinnacle Systems is especially concerning. He was one of the primary architects of Atlas 4.0's streaming engine and has deep knowledge of our real-time processing approach. While he signed an NDA and non-compete (limited to 6 months for California employees), the knowledge transfer risk is real.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Immediate (July):**
   - Announce Atlas 4.0 ship celebration: company-wide day off + team recognition event
   - Engineering mandatory PTO: require all Atlas 4.0 team members to take minimum 5 consecutive days off in July or August
   - Emergency spot bonuses for top 20 Atlas 4.0 contributors ($5K-15K range, total budget: $150K)
   - Accelerate VP of Product hire — make it priority #1 for CEO's calendar

2. **Near-Term (Q3):**
   - Establish engineering capacity model — no more crunch commitments without VP Eng + CTO approval
   - Create PM career ladder (PM1 → PM2 → Senior PM → Group PM → Director → VP) with published criteria
   - Conduct stay interviews with all engineers and PMs rated as flight risks (estimated 25-30 individuals)
   - Implement "no meeting Wednesdays" for engineering and product teams

3. **Medium-Term (Q4):**
   - Board to review comprehensive retention plan at October meeting
   - Evaluate equity refresh program for retention (compensation committee action)
   - Assess whether Product should be restructured (separate Product Management from Product Design orgs)
   - Consider engineering team restructuring post-Atlas 4.0 to reduce single-team dependency
