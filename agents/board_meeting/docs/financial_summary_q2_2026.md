# Meridian Technologies — Q2 2026 Financial Summary

**Prepared by:** Rachel Morrison, Chief Financial Officer
**Date:** July 2, 2026
**Classification:** Board Confidential

---

## Executive Summary

Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $52.1M, representing 10.1% quarter-over-quarter growth and 32.2% year-over-year growth. This was the strongest quarterly revenue in company history and exceeded the internal forecast of $50.5M by $1.6M. The outperformance was driven primarily by enterprise segment strength and accelerating Beacon Analytics adoption.

However, the quarter also saw significant operating expense overruns totaling $2.4M above budget, driven by Atlas 4.0 delay-related costs, accelerated APAC investment, and higher-than-expected legal expenses. Operating margin contracted from 12.9% in Q1 to 9.8% in Q2, and free cash flow turned negative for the first time in three quarters.

The board should note the tension between our growth trajectory (which remains strong) and our cost discipline (which weakened meaningfully in Q2). Management is committed to bringing OpEx growth back in line with revenue growth by Q4 2026.

### Beacon Analytics

Beacon Analytics continues to be the standout growth story. Q2 highlights:

- **Revenue:** $3.2M (up from $1.8M in Q1, 77.8% growth)
- **Paying Customers:** 35 (up from 23 in Q1)
- **Average Deal Size:** $91K ARR (up from $78K in Q1 — mix shift toward mid-market)
- **Cross-sell Rate:** 41% of new Beacon customers are existing Atlas customers
- **Standalone Win Rate:** 62% in competitive evaluations

Top Beacon use cases by revenue:

1. Financial fraud detection — $890K (28% of Beacon revenue)
2. Operational anomaly detection — $720K (23%)
3. Customer behavior analytics — $640K (20%)
4. Supply chain disruption alerts — $510K (16%)
5. IT infrastructure monitoring — $440K (14%)

The Beacon 2.0 roadmap (ML-powered anomaly detection) is expected to significantly expand the addressable market, particularly in healthcare and cybersecurity verticals where pattern recognition is critical.

### Key Expense Trends

**Compensation & Benefits:** $32.8M (69.8% of OpEx, up from 68.2% in Q1). Headcount grew from 847 to 891 (+44 net). Average fully-loaded cost per employee: $36.8K/month.

**Contractors & Professional Services:** $5.1M (10.9% of OpEx, up from 7.8% in Q1). The increase was almost entirely driven by Atlas 4.0 contractor surge. With 4.0 shipping, contractor spend should decline by $1.2M/quarter starting Q3.

**Infrastructure & Cloud:** $4.8M (10.2% of OpEx). Singapore datacenter build completion ($3.1M was CapEx, not OpEx). Ongoing cloud costs: $4.8M, up 8% from Q1 due to increased enterprise data volumes.

**Legal:** $1.3M total in H1 for patent dispute. Run-rate appears to be $500-600K/quarter through trial. Total estimated spend: $3-5M.

### Metric Definitions

- **Net Revenue Retention:** Revenue from customers who were active 12 months ago, including expansion and contraction, divided by their revenue 12 months ago. Excludes churned customers.
- **Gross Churn:** Annualized revenue lost from churned customers as a percentage of beginning-of-period ARR.
- **Rule of 40:** Sum of annualized revenue growth rate and operating margin. Industry benchmark for SaaS health.
